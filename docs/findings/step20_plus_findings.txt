Step 31: what the script does

I combined the control work into one run.

1.  Universe. It downloads the SEC's financial statement data sets for 2019 Q1 and Q2 (sub.txt only) and keeps US retail-SIC 10-K filers. That is SIC 5200–5499, 5531, 5600–5799 and 5900–5999, with restaurants and car dealers excluded.

2.  Size gate. It downloads each company's facts and keeps those with annual revenue between 250m and 20bn, set from the failed companies' sales. The design-sample and held-out failed companies are left out of the new batch.

3.  Events. It checks every company in range for any Item 1.03 8-K since 2014. A hit marks a NEW FAILED company. That removes a flaw I should have caught earlier: my curated failed list could miss bankruptcies, and an unlisted failure would sit in the control group as if healthy.

4.  Extraction and debt tie-out.

    -   For each company it checks core data coverage: revenue, cost or gross profit, and inventory must each cover at least 85% of quarters since 2014.

    -   It tests 17 candidate debt formulas against the balance-sheet debt lines in the filings at 6 sampled quarters. It picks the best-agreeing one, and the company passes if at least 5 of 6 tie and 80% of quarters are covered.

    -   If a company has no debt facts and no debt lines in the sampled filings, it counts as zero debt (call this rule R2).

5.  Output: one line per company, then a summary, and files controls_summary.csv and universe_size_gate.csv.
